The challenge

Almost every career resource for accounting students stops at the offer letter. Explore the career, win the scholarship, pass the exam, land the internship, and then silence. School teaches the accounting; nobody teaches the rest. Firms, meanwhile, were describing the same first-year gaps to each other and not to the students.

The approach

I built the package from direct conversations with the people who lead and hire at CPA firms, then grounded each claim in the published research. The paper names the practices that hold up, grades the evidence behind each one, and names the famous statistics nobody should be citing. It travels with a set of pages written for each audience: the real recruiting calendar for students, a 12-behavior scorecard for the first 90 days, a six-move onboarding plan for firms that run without a training department, and a classroom session for professors.

Four doors, one body of evidence

Students, new hires, firm leaders, and professors each get a page that hands them something usable the same day, all drawn from the same graded research.

Where it stands

The second package in the Insights series, delivered to state CPA societies for their own branding, with a partner webinar and a classroom offer alongside.
