Director of Strategic Research and Insights, PICPA and CPA Crossings. Philadelphia.

I run the research operation at the Pennsylvania Institute of CPAs and its national research arm, CPA Crossings. The papers I write there go out under the names of state CPA societies across the country.

Before this I spent a decade in cognitive science, studying how people learn, attend, and decide. That turned out to be the right preparation for the question most organizations are now working through: what happens when skilled people are handed AI, and what it takes for them to keep using it.

The firms that navigate this period best will not be the firms that trusted everything. They will be the firms that started from trust and built from there.
