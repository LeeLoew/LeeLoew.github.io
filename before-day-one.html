The classmates who collect offers senior year are usually the ones who started as underclassmen, and the window opens far sooner than campus rumor says it does. Below is the actual calendar, and one move per year. Find your row.

The real recruiting calendar

4years out
Freshman

Early-identification programs quietly recruit underclassmen; several open as early as freshman year.

Show up: accounting club, one career fair, and ask your career center to connect you with a CPA to shadow for a day.

3years out
Sophomore

Early-ID internships and leadership programs are posted for students two or more years from graduation.

Search each target firm's early-careers page for its early-ID program by name, and apply to at least one.

2years out
Junior

Applications for the internship before senior year open in August and September, roughly a year ahead of the internship itself.

Put the application window on your calendar for the first week of fall classes, and treat it like a deadline.

1year out
Senior

The internship converts, or does not, into the full-time offer, usually decided by summer's end.

Treat the internship as the tryout it is, then start the exam while school is fresh.

If the calendar already passed you

The multi-year machinery above belongs mostly to the largest firms. Many small and mid-size firms hire much closer to their start dates, and an internship at a small local firm that you take seriously will do more for you than a bigger name you never applied to. Small Firm, Mid-Size, Large explains why that path is often the better one anyway.

The licensure piece

Most states have added alternative pathways to CPA licensure since 2025. In plain terms: the traditional 150-credit-hour route is no longer the only door in much of the country; many states now also license CPAs through a bachelor's degree plus additional professional experience, and the rules keep changing state by state. Which pathway you plan around changes how you pick your credits, when you can sit for the exam, and how soon after graduation you can hold the license. Do not build your final year on what an older student or a recruiting page remembers; check [SOCIETY NAME] for your state's current requirements before you lock in your schedule, and ask what each pathway looks like where you plan to practice.
